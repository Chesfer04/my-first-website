About Me

Hey there! I’m Chesfer Andrew V. Briagas, a 3rd-year BSIT-Animation student from Bicol University with a passion for turning imagination into visuals.

I love creating 3D models, animations, and digital art—each piece I make is a small step in bringing stories to life. Whether it’s designing characters, sculpting forms, or animating movement, I enjoy the process of making ideas move and breathe.

For me, animation isn’t just about visuals—it’s about emotion, creativity, and connection. I’m always exploring new techniques, learning from every project, and pushing myself to grow as a digital artist.

Welcome to my world of art, where every frame tells a story. ✨
